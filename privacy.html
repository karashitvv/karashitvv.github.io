Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ClipForge runs on the owner's own computer. It has no servers, user accounts or advertising, and it does not sell or share data.

When a TikTok or YouTube account is connected, ClipForge receives: basic profile information (display name, avatar), an access token, and statistics for videos posted from that account (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time).

This data is stored only on the owner's computer and is used only to upload the user's own videos and to show their performance inside the app.

Videos are sent directly from the computer to TikTok or YouTube. No data is sent to any other third party.

ClipForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove access, disconnect inside ClipForge or revoke the app in your TikTok or Google account settings. Local data can be deleted by deleting the app's data folder.
